About

Why nature matters

Canopy is a simple guide for people who want to spend more time outside. We're not about extreme adventures or expensive gear — just forests, mountains, lakes, and the quiet that comes with them.

Whether you're five minutes from a local park or a full day's drive from the wilderness, there's always somewhere worth exploring.
